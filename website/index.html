Can multiple people take inventory at once? +

Yes. Each person works in their own inventory session, so separate counts don't overwrite one another.

Does Invotra work with barcode scanners? +

Invotra is designed for barcode-based inventory and browser-based scanning workflows, including supported Zebra devices configured for keyboard input. Hardware setup should be tested for each device model.

What happens when an item isn't recognized? +

The person counting provides a specific product name and suggested GL account. An admin reviews the item details and pricing before final approval.

How do I get an account? +

Invotra access is managed by administrators. Reach out to info@invotra.us to discuss access.
